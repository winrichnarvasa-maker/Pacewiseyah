How to open:
1. Unzip the folder.
2. Double-click index.html (Google Chrome is recommended).
3. No internet, server, or installation is needed.

Works on phones, tablets, laptops, and desktops. On a wide screen the
six navigation buttons move to a green sidebar on the left. Follows the device's light/dark mode.

Files:
index.html  - page structure (six pages: Home, Tasks, Focus, Progress, Mood, Settings)
style.css   - layout and design (phone, tablet, and desktop)
js/helpers.js   - small helper functions (ids, dates, messages)
js/storage.js   - appData, localStorage, cleaning data, backup and restore
js/tasks.js     - tasks, steps, Today's Plan limit, search and filters
js/progress.js  - wins, focus minutes, weekly summary, Home and Progress pages
js/timer.js     - focus timer and breaks
js/mood.js      - mood check-in
js/settings.js  - settings and clear all data
js/app.js       - page navigation, event listeners, start-up (loaded last)

Main features:
1. Task list (add, edit, delete) with subject and due date
2. Small steps with a progress bar and count
3. Today's Plan (up to 3 tasks)
4. Focus page: timer with a circle that shrinks (Start/Pause, Skip, Reset) and today's focus numbers
5. Break reminder (longer break after every 4th round)
6. Small Wins and Completed tasks (on the Progress page)
7. Mood page: daily mood, short note, week row and mood history
8. Weekly summary on the Progress page (tasks, steps, minutes, sessions, best day, chart)
9. Search and filters (status and subject)
10. Auto save, backup file, restore file, and clear all data
11. Home page: greeting, today's summary, and shortcuts
12. Optional name for the greeting (Settings)

Notes:
- The timer only runs while the page is open.
- Data is saved in this browser. Use Settings > Backup file to keep a copy.
